Display interpretation

Communities group unsigned retained connectivity. They are descriptive, not established functional modules. Gene colours describe broad eggNOG functions where available.

Gene-edge widths describe representative locus |β|. Community-link widths count retained gene edges, without a pooled P-value or effect. Positions are diagram coordinates, not genomic coordinates.

White matrix cells mean no retained map edge. Untested and nonsignificant pairs cannot be distinguished from this map.
